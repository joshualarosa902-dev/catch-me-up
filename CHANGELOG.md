# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-19)

First version.

- `/catch-me-up <topic>` with a time window (default 24h).
- Reads Hacker News, YouTube, Bluesky, Google News, GitHub, Mastodon and Product Hunt with no
  account or key. Tries Reddit once and reports when it is blocked.
- Drops posts outside the window, duplicates, and posts already shown for the same topic.
- Optional web search step for X, TikTok and Reddit, labeled as such.
